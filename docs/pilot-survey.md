# ProofPerks pilot survey

Ask immediately after the claim session. Do not ask for a name, email, wallet address, contributor secret, or transaction hash. An optional facilitator-only label such as `P01` may be kept separately from responses if needed for scheduling; do not put it in the metrics export.

## Claim-flow feedback

For questions 1–6, use a 1–5 scale:

`1 = strongly disagree` · `3 = neither` · `5 = strongly agree`

1. I understood what I needed to do to claim the reward.
2. Connecting or using my wallet was straightforward.
3. I understood which information stayed private.
4. I trusted that the claim could not be used twice.
5. The proof/wait time felt acceptable.
6. The success or failure message was clear.

7. How difficult was the overall claim flow?  
   `Very easy / Easy / Neutral / Difficult / Very difficult`

8. What was the most confusing step?

9. What would make you more confident using this for a real community reward?

10. Would you use ProofPerks again for another campaign?  
    `Definitely yes / Probably yes / Unsure / Probably no / Definitely no`

11. Optional: anything else we should change before Wave 2?

## Facilitator-only observation prompts

Record these separately from survey answers and without participant identifiers:

- Wallet connection completed without intervention? `yes/no`
- Claim completed on first attempt? `yes/no`
- Participant asked where the secret or points go? `yes/no`
- Participant needed an explanation of the issuer trust boundary? `yes/no`
- Observable blocker category: `wallet`, `network`, `privacy explanation`, `claim instructions`, `other`
